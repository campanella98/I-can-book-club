Frequently Asked Questions & Overview
What is "Mattheus, The Only Child" about?
Mattheus, The Only Child is a children's picture book by Alessandro Campanella that explores the unique perspective, imagination, and personal journey of being an only child.
Who is the target audience for this book?
The book is written for young readers, families, and educators looking for engaging children's stories that touch on family dynamics, self-discovery, and imagination.
Where can I purchase or find "Mattheus, The Only Child"?
The book is published and available internationally via Amazon and major book distributors. Physical deposit copies are also held in Canadian institutional libraries including Library and Archives Canada and Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec (BAnQ).
Who is Alessandro Campanella?
Alessandro Campanella is a Canadian author, screenwriting contributor, and mathematics graduate based in Quebec, Canada.
